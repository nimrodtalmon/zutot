The perfect-round bonus

If we assume no noise, then a solution exists

Each accepted paper returns its credit; if all are accepted, then +1 bonus

But with noise, it breaks

Reviewers err, say, 1 time in 5: the more you send, the rarer a perfect round

The fix: forgive what noise explains

Only rejections beyond what noise plausibly causes cost a credit
