Profile

Tenured Associate Professor of Computer Science (Ben-Gurion University) and industry consultant on blockchain/DAO governance, voting systems, and mechanism design. Combines a leading academic record in computational social choice (2,900+ citations, h-index 30; AAMAS/IJCAI/AAAI) with hands-on governance work for major blockchain ecosystems including Cardano (IOG), Optimism, and DAOstack. Advises on governance mechanism design and evaluation: voting-power distribution, delegation, treasury and funding allocation (quadratic and retroactive funding), incentive design, and attack/bribery resistance — as well as AI governance and multi-agent AI systems.
